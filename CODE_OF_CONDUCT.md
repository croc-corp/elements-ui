# Code of Conduct

LumeSec Elements adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions and any other project space, and when someone represents the project in public.

## In short

Expected:

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is binding; this summary does not replace it.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code of conduct to **hello@lumesec.ai**. Reports are read by the project
maintainers at LumeSec Technologies GmbH, who respect the privacy and security of the person reporting.

Maintainers decide on the consequences following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1:
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban.
